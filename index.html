I) Course Description

This course provides an introduction to the basic elements of English grammar and writing skills. The course also includes active vocabulary that provides students with the necessary language to answer the topic questions that are presented and practiced.

Many activities are set up in different ways, according to the needs of students and teachers, fostering a dynamic and communicative environment for young learners.

📖

Foundational Language

Focuses on building fundamental English grammar and structured writing skills for 3rd-grade students.

🗣️

Active Vocabulary

Provides essential vocabulary tools to answer thematic topic questions with clarity and confidence.

🎯

Flexible Methodology

Activities tailored to diverse learning styles, supporting individual growth and collaborative learning.

III) Academic Competencies

Students consolidate their ability to understand, use and produce English in familiar communicative situations. They listen to, read and respond to simple oral and written messages with growing independence, recognizing different phonemes, intonation and text types.

They speak and write short texts, apply basic grammatical structures with confidence, and integrate verbal and non-verbal elements to communicate ideas, needs and opinions. Additionally, they show an appreciation of English as a tool for intercultural communication and engage cooperatively in meaningful language learning tasks.

IV) Achievement Indicators

1

Listens to and carries out instructions in English that involve familiar contexts and identifies the required action.

2

Distinguishes English phonemes that differ from their native language and attempts accurate pronunciation of short phrases.

3

Reads short texts in English (commands, dialogues, announcements) and responds appropriately by action or verbal reply.

4

Uses short sentences and phrases in English to ask and answer simple questions about familiar topics (e.g., classroom objects, activities, preferences).

5

Writes brief texts (such as notes, descriptions or commands) in English using vocabulary and grammar learned and shows awareness of basic conventions (capitalization, punctuation).

6

Uses verbal and non-verbal strategies (gestures, drawings, body language) to support meaning when speaking or listening in English.

7

Demonstrates positive attitude towards English, shows willingness to attempt new language tasks, cooperate with peers and reflect on the use of English in diverse contexts.

V) Institutional Didactic Methodology

Colegio Montano applies an active-collaborative model integrating Constructivism, Case Studies, Flipped Classroom, and Project-Based Learning (PBL). Lessons follow the structured Double Period Method (70 minutes) aligned with the Four Moments Methodology.

Double Period Structure (70 Minutes)

10-15 Min

1. Motivating Introduction

Prior knowledge activation through educational games, energizers, warm-ups, and brainstorming sessions.

15-20 Min

2. Individual Work

Development of personal potential, reading comprehension, writing skills, and individual analytical thinking.

20-25 Min

3. Teamwork

Collaborative interaction with defined group roles under the core principle: "All for one and one for all."

10-15 Min

4. Plenary Session

Public presentations, Socratic dialogue, group synthesis, and assignment of prior home reading.

Four Moments Methodology & Habits

Aligns lessons with key habits for self-leadership and proactive habit development across all reading activities.

Flipped Classroom Model

Empowers students to prepare content in advance at home, maximizing interactive classroom time for collaborative practice.
